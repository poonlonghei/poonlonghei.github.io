## A life of faith, _story, and service._

My name is Chavez Poon, and I am a young Traditionalist Roman Catholic layman, Acolyte, and Liturgical Master of Ceremonies. I am also a member of the Society of St. Justin Martyr (Societas Sancti Iustini Martyris, SSIM) in the United Kingdom. As a Catholic deeply devoted to the Sacred Liturgy and the historic traditions of the Roman Catholic Church, I seek to serve the Church and communicate the beauty and richness of her faith through liturgical service, writing, journalism, and digital media.

### My Journey of Faith

I was born in June 2003 in Hong Kong, where I spent the first twenty-two years of my life. I was baptized into the Roman Catholic Church in April 2012 and received the Sacrament of Confirmation in October 2014. My Catholic faith has remained a central part of my life, shaping my values, aspirations, and understanding of my vocation to serve God and His Church.

In December 2025, I moved to the United Kingdom to begin working at the Basilica and National Shrine of Our Lady of Walsingham, England's historic national Marian shrine in North Norfolk, affectionately known as England's Nazareth. During my time at the Shrine, I had the privilege of assisting pilgrims from across the United Kingdom and around the world as they came to Walsingham to offer their prayers and intentions to Our Blessed Lady. This experience deepened my appreciation for the spiritual significance of pilgrimage, the universality of the Catholic Church, and the enduring devotion of the faithful to the Mother of God.

In May 2026, after a period of prayer and discernment, I came to recognise that God was calling me towards a different path and mission. I subsequently left my position at the Walsingham Shrine and embarked on a new chapter of my life as an independent freelance writer and Catholic social media content creator. Although my professional direction changed, my commitment to serving the Church remained steadfast. I came to see that the gifts and experiences God had entrusted to me could also be put to use through Catholic journalism, storytelling, and digital evangelisation.

### Catholic Media and Journalism

Since June 2026, I have also contributed to EWTN Great Britain as a Production Assistant, supporting the production of Catholic media through filming, video editing, interviews, and event coverage at major Catholic gatherings across the United Kingdom. This work has allowed me to contribute to the communication of the Church's life and mission, while gaining valuable experience in media production and Catholic broadcasting.

Alongside my work with EWTN Great Britain, I pursue independent writing and digital content creation, with particular interests in the Sacred Liturgy, the historic and traditional practices of the Roman Rite, ecclesiastical history, and contemporary Catholic affairs. Through my writing and media projects, I seek to explore the Church's rich liturgical heritage, document significant ecclesiastical events, and share informed perspectives on developments within the Catholic world.

My work is guided by a desire to communicate the truth with charity, to preserve an appreciation for the Church's historical and liturgical heritage, and to make the treasures of the Catholic tradition accessible to a wider audience. I believe that Catholic media can serve not merely as a means of reporting events, but also as a means of education, evangelisation, and fostering a deeper understanding of the life of the Church.

### Liturgical Service and Ceremonial Experience

Beyond my work in media and journalism, I bring more than ten years of ceremonial and liturgical experience, having served as an Acolyte, Parish Sacristan, and Liturgical Master of Ceremonies at Holy Mass and other sacred liturgical celebrations across Hong Kong, Macau, and the United Kingdom.

These years of service have given me a particular appreciation for the dignity, order, symbolism, and spiritual depth of the Sacred Liturgy. I am especially interested in the historical development of the Roman Rite, the ceremonial traditions of the Church, and the ways in which the liturgy expresses and transmits the Catholic faith across generations.

For me, liturgical service is more than the fulfilment of ceremonial duties. It is an opportunity to serve Christ and His Church with reverence, discipline, humility, and attention to detail. My experience in the sacristy and sanctuary has also shaped my approach to Catholic media, encouraging me to approach ecclesiastical subjects with care, historical awareness, and respect for the sacred realities they represent.

My interests and experience therefore lie at the intersection of Catholic media, journalism, digital content creation, ecclesiastical history, and the Sacred Liturgy. I am particularly passionate about bringing together these fields to communicate the beauty, richness, history, and living tradition of the Roman Catholic Church.

### Faith, Gratitude, and Providence

Of course, not every day is a good day. There have been times when I have returned home disappointed, exhausted, or distressed by the challenges I have encountered because of my Catholic faith, ethnicity, skin colour, and Hong Kong Chinese background. Living and working in a new country has not always been easy, and there have been moments when I have had to face difficulties that tested my patience, resilience, and trust in God.

Nevertheless, when I look back upon my life, I increasingly recognise how God has been preparing my path in ways I could not always understand at the time. What once appeared to be an unexpected change of direction has sometimes become an opportunity for growth, while experiences of uncertainty have taught me to place greater confidence in Divine Providence. Through every challenge, transition, and new beginning, I believe that God has continued to guide me, often through the kindness and generosity of the people He has placed in my life.

For this, I give heartfelt thanks to God for all those who have supported me, loved me, comforted me, encouraged me, and remembered me in their prayers. Their friendship and kindness have been a source of strength, particularly during difficult moments. I am equally grateful for the opportunities I have received, the lessons I have learned, the experiences that have shaped me, and the gifts that God has entrusted to me.

Above all, I give thanks to God for all that I have and all that I am. My journey of faith, my years of service to the Church, and my continuing discernment of God's will have taught me that a vocation is not simply about choosing a particular career or following a predetermined plan. It is about remaining attentive to God's call and being willing to follow wherever He may lead.

I continue to entrust my future to the Sacred Heart of Jesus and the Immaculate Heart of Mary, asking for the grace to serve the Church faithfully, to use my talents for the greater glory of God, and to persevere in faith, hope, and charity. Whatever the future may hold, I hope to look back upon my life with gratitude, knowing that even through its trials and uncertainties, God's grace has never ceased to sustain me.
