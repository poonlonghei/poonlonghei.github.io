About Chavez

Chavez Poon is a Roman Catholic freelance writer and social media content creator, born and raised in Hong Kong and now based in Walsingham, Norfolk. His work explores the Sacred Liturgy, the historic traditions of the Roman Catholic Church, ecclesiastical history, and contemporary Catholic life.

He works as a Production Assistant with EWTN Great Britain, supporting the filming, interviews, and media coverage of Catholic events across the United Kingdom. In 2026, he also took part in the EWTN Summer Academy in Rome, developing his interests in Catholic journalism, digital evangelisation, and media communications.

Chavez brings more than ten years of ceremonial and liturgical experience to this work. He has served as an Acolyte, Sacristan, and Master of Ceremonies at Mass and other liturgical celebrations in parishes and ecclesial communities across Hong Kong, Macau, and the United Kingdom.

He is also an amateur website designer and developer, using this site to bring together his writing, media projects, and his personal hobbies and interests.

Through his work and liturgical service, he hopes to share the beauty of the Church’s worship, the continuity of Roman Catholic tradition, and the message of the Gospel, while contributing to the preservation and communication of the Church’s heritage.
