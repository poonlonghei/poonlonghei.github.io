New Website

Time to start again

By Chavez Cyrillus Mariae Poon

A journey that began in 2020

My journey in building a personal website began in 2020, when I first started exploring the possibilities of creating my own space on the internet. What began as an opportunity to learn about web design and establish an online presence gradually developed into a long-term personal project. Over the years, I have continued to experiment with different technologies, refine my ideas, and discover new ways to present my work and interests online.

As my skills and professional interests developed, so did my vision for the website. It became more than just a personal webpage: it evolved into a place where I could introduce myself, share my writing, showcase my projects, and document my involvement in the life of the Roman Catholic Church. Looking back, every iteration of the website represents a different stage of my learning, creativity, and personal development.

A fresh start

Starting again with a website is an opportunity to reconsider what I want it to achieve. My earlier versions served their purpose at different stages of my journey, but I eventually realised that I wanted something more focused, professional, and reflective of who I am today.

I initially built this project using WordPress, which allowed me to establish a website without having to develop every component from scratch. It was a practical starting point, providing the flexibility to publish content and organise different aspects of my online presence. As the website grew, however, I began to envision it more clearly as a professional portfolio: a central place to introduce myself, present my work, and connect visitors with my various websites, publications, and media projects.

This new chapter is therefore not simply about redesigning a website. It is about rethinking how I present myself online and creating a more coherent home for my professional work, personal interests, and continuing journey.

Moving beyond WordPress

WordPress served as a valuable starting point, but as my requirements evolved, I found myself increasingly dependent on plugins and additional tools to achieve the layouts and functionality I wanted. Managing these components, together with the increasingly complex editing experience, gradually became more involved than necessary for a relatively straightforward personal portfolio.

I wanted greater control over the structure, presentation, and performance of my website. Returning to the fundamentals of web development gave me an opportunity to approach the project from a different perspective, with a clearer understanding of how individual components work together.

Working directly with HTML and CSS helped me appreciate the simplicity and flexibility of building pages from the ground up. Rather than adapting my ideas to the limitations of a particular theme or plugin, I could shape the design around my own needs, maintain a lightweight structure, and create a more consistent experience across the website.

For me, this transition also represents an important part of my development as a web creator: moving beyond relying solely on ready-made solutions towards understanding and building the tools that bring my ideas to life.

Building with purpose

The latest iteration of my website takes a more modern approach, using TypeScript and Next.js to bring together my professional portfolio, writing, media projects, liturgical service, and personal interests within a unified platform.

My aim is to create a website that is not only visually clear and accessible, but also practical and purposeful. Visitors should be able to learn about my background, explore my published work, discover my projects, and find the appropriate channels for professional enquiries and collaboration without navigating several disconnected platforms.

The website also reflects the different dimensions of my work. As an independent freelance writer and Catholic content creator, I am interested in Catholic journalism, ecclesiastical history, the Sacred Liturgy, and digital media. Bringing these interests together in one place allows me to present a more complete picture of my work while continuing to develop my skills in web design and technology.

Of course, building a website is an ongoing process. There will always be opportunities to improve its design, introduce new features, and make the experience better for visitors. I regard this version not as a finished product, but as another milestone in a journey that began in 2020.

Looking ahead

Over the past several years, my personal website has evolved alongside my skills, interests, and professional aspirations. Each redesign has given me an opportunity to learn something new, reconsider my priorities, and find better ways to communicate who I am and what I do.

This latest rebuild is a return to the foundations of that journey, combining what I have learned with a clearer vision for the future. It is an opportunity to build something more personal, maintainable, and closely aligned with my work.

For now, this website is a fresh starting point: a place to bring my work together, share what I am learning, and continue exploring the intersection of technology, creativity, and my service to the Church.

The journey that began in 2020 continues, and I look forward to seeing where it leads next.
