Born and raised in Hong Kong and now based in Walsingham, Norfolk, I work across Catholic media, writing, content creation, and liturgical service. My interests include the Sacred Liturgy, the history and traditions of the Roman Catholic Church, and contemporary Catholic life.

I’m a Production Assistant with EWTN Great Britain and have more than ten years of ceremonial and liturgical experience. I also enjoy building this website and learning how thoughtful digital design can help good work reach people.
